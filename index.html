    🧠 How the prediction works

    For every token we simulate the counters forward: each counter's remaining time plus the average service time of each person ahead is assigned to whichever counter frees up first.

    That is multiplied by a live learning factor (exponential moving average of actual vs. expected service time), so the estimate self-corrects when officers are faster or slower than usual.

    Priority tokens jump ahead of regular ones. Confidence band widens with queue position.

    🚦 Smart alerts

    Get ready – wait ≤ travel time + 25 min.
    Leave now – wait ≤ travel time + 8 min.
    Proceed to counter – when your token is called.

    Missed your slot? A token not reached within 10 min of being called is skipped and moved back.
